About HmongHeritage

A community-driven initiative to preserve, teach, and celebrate Hmong language, culture, and history for generations to come.

Our Mission

The HmongHeritage project was created to address a critical need: the rapid loss of Hmong language and cultural knowledge among younger generations of the diaspora.

As Hmong communities spread across the globe — from the United States and France to Australia and Argentina — the connections that sustain our language and traditions face unprecedented challenges.

Our mission is simple but profound: to provide accessible, accurate, and respectful resources that help Hmong people of all ages stay connected to their heritage.

🌏

Hmong Around the World

From the mountains of China and Southeast Asia to communities in the Americas, Europe, and Oceania — the Hmong diaspora spans the globe, but our culture remains one people.

Hmong History Timeline

A brief overview of the Hmong journey through time.

Ancient Origins

Hmong people trace their origins to ancient China, particularly the Yellow River region. Rich oral traditions speak of a great civilization that migrated southward over millennia.

Migration to Southeast Asia

Over centuries, Hmong communities settled in the mountainous regions of China, Vietnam, Laos, and Thailand. They developed distinctive agricultural practices and a rich cultural identity adapted to highland life.

Laos & The Secret War (1950s–1975)

During the Vietnam War, Hmong people in Laos played a crucial role alongside American forces in what became known as "The Secret War." This period profoundly shaped modern Hmong identity and led to mass displacement.

Refugee Crisis (1975–1990s)

After the Communist takeover of Laos, hundreds of thousands of Hmong fled to refugee camps in Thailand. Many were resettled in third countries, creating the modern Hmong diaspora.

Diaspora & Resettlement (1975–Present)

Hmong communities established themselves in the United States (California, Wisconsin, Minnesota), France,French Guiana, Australia, Argentina, and elsewhere — rebuilding lives while preserving culture.

Cultural Preservation Today

Today, Hmong communities worldwide are working to maintain their language, traditions, and identity. Digital tools, education programs, and cultural organizations are helping bridge the gap between generations.

Why This Matters

Language and culture are not just heritage — they are the foundation of identity, belonging, and resilience.

🗣️

Language Loss

Studies show that Hmong language proficiency drops dramatically by the third generation of diaspora. Without active preservation efforts, Hmong could face the same fate as many endangered languages worldwide.

🧬

Cultural Identity

Hmong culture — from Paj Ntaub embroidery to qeej music, from spirit ceremonies to New Year celebrations — provides a unique worldview and sense of belonging that shapes who Hmong people are.

📚

Education Gap

There are very few structured Hmong language learning resources available. This project aims to fill that gap with comprehensive, accessible lessons for learners of all ages and backgrounds.

🤝

Community Building

By connecting Hmong people worldwide through shared knowledge and resources, we strengthen the bonds that keep our culture alive — no matter where in the world we call home.
