What We Offer

Comprehensive resources to help you learn and preserve Hmong language and culture.

📚

Hmong Language Lessons

From basic greetings to advanced grammar — structured lessons for all levels. Learn Hmong Njua (Green Hmong) and Hmong Daw (White Hmong) dialects with pronunciation guides.

🎭

Culture & Traditions

Discover Hmong festivals, clothing traditions, music, dance, and the deep spiritual practices that have sustained the Hmong people for centuries across generations.

📜

History & Migration

Trace the remarkable journey of the Hmong people — from ancient China through Southeast Asia to communities across the Americas, Europe, and beyond.

🌏

Community Resources

Connect with Hmong communities worldwide. Find events, organizations, language programs, and cultural centers dedicated to preserving Hmong identity.

🎨

Paj Ntaub (Story Cloth)

Learn about the intricate art of Hmong story cloth embroidery — a powerful tradition of visual storytelling that documents Hmong history and daily life.

🎵

Hmong Music & Poetry

Explore the rich tradition of Hmong folk songs, polyphonic chanting, and the unique poetic forms like "Yaw Noj Yaw Ntoo" that express Hmong worldview.
